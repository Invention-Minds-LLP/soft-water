## Users

Primary: Karnataka homeowners, typically a Kannada-speaking family building or moving into a new house that draws water from a newly drilled borewell. They discover the water is hard or unsafe (white scale on taps and tiles, stiff clothes, dry hair and skin, bad taste, stained buckets) and are looking for someone to test and treat it.

Secondary: small commercial and residential-complex buyers (apartments, offices, hotels) who need softening, filtration, RO or STP/ETP at a larger scale.

Their job: understand what is wrong with their water, trust that this company can fix it, and call or WhatsApp for a water test and installation.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website for the client's water-treatment business, branded **Soft Water Technologies**. It exists to turn a worried homeowner into an enquiry (call, WhatsApp, or contact form for a free water test). Success = enquiries.

The site tells one story the visitor recognises from their own life: a Kannada family builds their new home, drills a borewell, finds the water is hard and impure and is heartbroken; Soft Water Technologies arrives, tests and treats the water, and the family's home becomes happy again.

## Positioning

Not a product catalogue. The business sells the end of a specific, familiar Karnataka disappointment: the new house is finished, the borewell is dug, and the water ruins it. Soft Water Technologies is the local expert who tests the water first and fits the right system for that borewell, then keeps servicing it.

## Operating Context

- The decision is family-level and emotional; the conversion action is a phone call or WhatsApp message, not online checkout.

## Product Principles

1. Tell the family's story first; products appear as the answer to it, not as a catalogue.
2. Every scene ends close to a call or WhatsApp action. One tap to enquire on mobile.
3. Never publish a claim the client has not confirmed; placeholders are visible and easy to swap.
4. Speak to Karnataka homes: Kannada where it carries feeling, English where it carries information.
5. Hard water is shown, not described: scale, stains, dull clothes, then clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Bilingual text: Kannada lines need a font with full Kannada script support and `lang="kn"` markup.
- Scroll animations must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and the story must remain readable without animation.
- Contact actions (tel:, WhatsApp) must be large tap targets on mobile.
