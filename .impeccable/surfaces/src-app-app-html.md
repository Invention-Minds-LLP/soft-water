# Surface brief: landing page (src/app)

Scope: single-page Angular marketing site for Soft Water Technologies. Mode: Persuade. Audience: Kannada-speaking Karnataka families with a new borewell home. Action: WhatsApp / call / enquiry form for a free water test. Story pinned by the user (new home, bore, hard water, sadness, Soft Water arrives, happiness). Motion style pinned: scroll-scrubbed pinned scenes like `E:\ai websites`. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the family's gruhapravesha water corner. Scroll performs the story from housewarming to borewell to hard water to softening to joy. It refuses the category default of a blue water-splash hero over a product grid.

OWN-WORLD: User-fused world: Gruhapravesha Kalasha (pick) + Steel Koda Row (roll). Drenched red-oxide floor (#7a2418) as the home ground; marigold (#f2a516), mango-leaf green (#2f6b2a), kumkum (#b3122e) for the housewarming; polished steel and copper kodas as the only "objects"; calcium-scale white crust as the enemy; a dusk indigo (#1b2340) for the sad act. Baloo Tamma 2 display for Kannada and English alike (hand-painted signboard warmth), Hind Mysuru body. Thoranam of mango leaves as section dividers, steel-tag labels for services.

STORY: Visitors see their own story, understand hard water through what it does to steel, see the softener fix it, and book a free water test on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Red-oxide ground. A mango-leaf and marigold thoranam swings across the top. Centre: an illustrated family of four at their new home's door, a copper kalasha and a row of polished steel kodas on the ledge in front. Headline "ಹೊಸ ಮನೆ, ಹೊಸ ನೀರು." with English line "Your new home deserves water as pure as the kalasha." WhatsApp "Book a free water test" primary plus a Call button, both in the first viewport on mobile.

FORM: Fused direction (roll #7 Steel Koda Row + pick Gruhapravesha Kalasha, user-requested). Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed scale rings crust over the steel kodas and the family's faces fall, then an ion-exchange particle canvas strips the hardness and the rings wipe clean as the family brightens. Raises: live water particle field (plankton); Kannada ಗಡಸು crusting and dissolving into ಮೃದು (alphabet storm); one unbroken pipe down the page as the timeline (step row). Seed key: 61f283cf.
